Financial Strategist & Co-Founder — Vanyug

Shark Tank Competition Project | Sustainable School Gardening Kits

Vanyug was conceived as an impact-driven venture engineered to deliver paintable, eco-friendly school gardening kits to primary students, fostering environmental stewardship and SDG alignment from an early age. Stepping into the role of Financial Strategist and Co-Founder, I spearheaded the complete unit economics, cost structures, and sales forecasts across multi-year expansion roadmaps. By meticulously analyzing raw inputs—including soil, bio-compost, customizable pots, and seeds—we established a weighted average cost price of Rs. 87.79 per kit against a wholesale selling price of Rs. 115.00, locking in a robust 23.7% gross profit margin. Our operational model projected supplying 10 partner schools scaling to 50 by 2029, targeting an annual volume of 10,500 kits and generating over Rs. 12 Lakhs in projected revenue. During the intense school pitch round against 12 competing teams, our precision financial modeling and value proposition clinched 1st Place, with one of the Shark judges so convinced that he purchased a kit on the spot for his daughter. Building on this momentum, we went on to represent the school at the state level among 20 elite teams, earning a prestigious Honourable Mention.
